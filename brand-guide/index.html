02 · Brand Strategy Foundations PROPOSED DIRECTION

Positioning, Audience, Voice & Visual Rationale

Positioning & Audience

Positioning: For Bangladeshi travellers-especially from Chattogram-who want guided South Asian & international tours without assembling logistics alone, Trip Express BD runs small-group departures with fixed dates, published ৳ prices, real trip photography, and human phone/office access.

Primary: Chattogram/greater-Chattogram adults (22–45), mobile-first, WhatsApp-native, bilingual. Secondary: Dhaka air-departure joiners. Tertiary: Corporate/friend-group custom trips.

Attributes, Mood & Voice

Attributes: Warm · Trustworthy · Energetic · Local · Proof-Driven.

Mood Words: Sunlit, Upward, Grounded, Communal, Transparent, Horizon-bound.

Voice & Microcopy: First-person communal ("our team", concrete counts like 7 travellers), honest exclusions, Bangla-primary itineraries + English chrome. Cultural expressions (Alhamdulillah, Insha'Allah) stay in trip reports, omitted from form errors.

Type Role & Scale Rules

  • Display / Wordmark: Plus Jakarta Sans (variable 700..800), tight tracking -0.02em to -0.03em.
  • Body / UI / Forms: Inter (variable 400..700, incl. 550/650), 16px body, 13px overline floor.
  • Bangla: Hind Siliguri (static 400;500;600;700), line-height: 1.85, letter-spacing: 0, never uppercase.
  • Data / Ledger: tabular-nums mandatory on prices, dates, seats; optional JetBrains Mono for booking & bank IDs.
