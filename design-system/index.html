06 · Accessibility Guidance

WCAG 2.2 AA Focus, Touch & Motion Rules

  • Focus Rings: 3px solid var(--te-focus-ring) with 2px offset (3px in High Contrast); overridden to #111823 on Sunset Orange buttons.
  • Touch Targets: Minimum 44×44px hit area via ::after even on compact 36px controls; minimum 8px spacing.
  • Status Clarity: Never rely on hue alone - pair icons/labels with status surfaces; input borders default to 3.95:1.
  • Motion Safety: @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) collapses durations to 0.01ms.
